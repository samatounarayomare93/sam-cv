## 3. EXPERIENCE SECTION

### ✅ Tips:
- Use **bullet points** (not paragraphs)
- Start each bullet with **action verbs**: Led, Managed, Resolved, Reduced, Achieved, Implemented
- Include **numbers** when possible: "50+ daily inquiries", "20% reduction"
- Add **context**: company size, team size, industry

### Example:
```
Customer Service Representative
Internet Service Provider | Keserwan
Feb 2023 – Mar 2025

• Resolved 50+ technical and billing inquiries daily with high first-contact resolution rate
• Recovered at-risk corporate revenue through proactive billing follow-up
• Decreased senior engineering ticket volume by 20% via standardized diagnostics
• Managed client accounts in CRM system (500+ active clients)
```
